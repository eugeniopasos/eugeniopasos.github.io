## Hello, I'm Eugenio 👋

I am an **Electrical & Robotics Engineer** driven by the challenge of designing intelligent physical systems from the transistor and firmware level all the way to high-level autonomous navigation and perception. 

Whether it is programming deterministic microsecond CAN communication between automotive ECUs, designing custom wiring harnesses and power distribution networks for an autonomous Mecanum rover, or translating human hand poses into robotic movements via computer vision, I thrive on building physical hardware that moves, thinks, and interacts intelligently with the real world.

## Hands-On Laboratory & Prototyping Toolkit

Building reliable robotics hardware requires rigorous benchtop testing and iteration. My development workflow incorporates:

* **Electronics & Test Equipment:** Rigol / Keysight digital storage oscilloscopes, USB logic analyzers for SPI/I2C/CAN bus decoding, benchtop DC power supplies with current-limiting, and precision SMD soldering rework stations.
* **Rapid Mechanical Prototyping:** 3D printing (FDM with PLA, PETG, and flexible TPU for custom vibration dampeners), CAD modeling for acrylic mounting decks, custom wire harnesses with crimped DuPont and JST connectors.
* **Software Stack:** Linux (Ubuntu 22.04 LTS), ROS 2 (Humble / Iron), C++17/20, Python 3, Bare-metal C, STM32CubeIDE, Docker containerization, Git & GitHub CI/CD workflows.
